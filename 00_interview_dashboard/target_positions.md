# Target Positions

This repository is used to prepare for research-oriented ML / AI interviews across multiple career tracks. Company names are intentionally omitted so that the preparation framework remains reusable.

## Current Target Tracks

1. **Research Internship**
   - Research fit and scientific reasoning
   - ML / DL fundamentals
   - Coding and implementation
   - Clear defense of prior research
   - Ability to reason about unfamiliar problems

2. **Industry Research Postdoc**
   - Research fit and technical depth
   - Strong research defense
   - ML / DL fundamentals
   - Experimental design and implementation
   - Ability to connect domain science with modern ML methods

3. **Academic Postdoc**
   - Research fit with the lab / PI
   - Publication-level understanding of prior work
   - Future research agenda
   - Scientific reasoning and experimental design
   - Ability to formulate tractable, high-impact research questions

4. **General Research Postdoc**
   - Research depth and independence
   - Research communication
   - Methodological rigor
   - Domain knowledge where relevant
   - Ability to propose and execute a coherent research program

## Default Preparation Strategy

When no position-specific information is available, use the following order:

1. Research fit
2. Prior research defense
3. Research reasoning and experimental design
4. ML / DL fundamentals
5. Implementation and coding
6. Position-specific topics such as LLMs, RL, ML systems, or domain science

For research internships, fundamentals and coding receive relatively more weight. For postdoctoral roles, research depth, research fit, scientific independence, and the future research agenda receive relatively more weight.

## Current ML Foundations Track

The current foundations review is:

```text
linear regression
    ↓
eigenvalues / eigenvectors / rank
    ↓
covariance
    ↓
PCA
    ↓
SVD
    ↓
low-rank approximation / pseudoinverse
```

The goal is not only to recognize formulas, but to be able to derive key results, explain the intuition, and answer follow-up questions in a research interview.
