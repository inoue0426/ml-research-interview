# Research Reasoning

既存の研究力を、open-ended interview question に対応できる形式に変換する。

## Topics
- Hypothesis generation
- Experimental design
- Ablation design
- Debugging models
- Interpreting results
- Distribution shift
- Negative results
- Research prioritization

## Practice Format

```text
Observation
  ↓
Possible hypotheses
  ↓
Discriminating experiments
  ↓
Expected outcomes
  ↓
Confounders
  ↓
Next decision
```

## Example Questions
- A 7B and 70B model have nearly identical validation performance. What would you investigate?
- Training loss improves but downstream performance gets worse. Why?
- An architectural change gives a small gain. How do you establish that the gain comes from the claimed mechanism?
