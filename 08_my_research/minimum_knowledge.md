# Minimum Knowledge — My Research Defense

各主要 project について、論文の要約ではなく**研究判断を defend できる状態**にする。

## Every Project Must Have
- [ ] one-sentence problem statement
- [ ] なぜ重要か
- [ ] prior work の限界
- [ ] central hypothesis
- [ ] key technical idea
- [ ] なぜその architecture / representation か
- [ ] 自分自身の contribution
- [ ] strongest result
- [ ] most important ablation
- [ ] major failure mode
- [ ] alternative approaches considered
- [ ] what I would change now
- [ ] next research question

## Technical Defense
各 project について以下に答える。
- [ ] Why this model?
- [ ] Why not a simpler baseline?
- [ ] Why not a Transformer?
- [ ] Why not a GNN?
- [ ] Is the gain just from more parameters?
- [ ] Is the gain just from extra data?
- [ ] How did you prevent leakage?
- [ ] How robust is it to distribution shift?
- [ ] Which assumption is most fragile?
- [ ] What experiment would falsify your hypothesis?

## Research Story
3段階で話せるようにする。
- [ ] 30 seconds: problem + contribution + result
- [ ] 2 minutes: motivation + method + result + significance
- [ ] 10 minutes: full technical discussion + limitations + future work

## Behavioral / Ownership
- [ ] hardest research decision
- [ ] failed experiment
- [ ] disagreement with collaborator
- [ ] debugging story
- [ ] project where hypothesis changed
- [ ] example of independent research direction
- [ ] example of prioritizing under limited compute/time

## Cross-project Narrative
- [ ] 自分の研究を一本の trajectory として説明
- [ ] representation learning / structured data / scientific discovery の接続
- [ ] なぜ frontier models / AI for Science に進むのか
- [ ] 過去の専門性が新領域でどう活きるか

## Rule
「何を作ったか」だけでなく、**なぜその判断をしたか / 何を学んだか / どこが弱いか**まで答える。
