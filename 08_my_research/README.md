# My Research

自分の研究を、論文要約ではなく interview defense 形式に変換する。

## Project Template

各プロジェクトについて以下を整理する。

```markdown
# Project

## Problem

## Why does this problem matter?

## Previous approaches

## My hypothesis

## Key technical idea

## Why this architecture / method?

## Alternatives considered

## My personal contribution

## Hardest technical problem

## Most surprising result

## Ablations

## Failure cases

## What I would change now

## Follow-up research

## Interview attacks

Q: Why did you choose this model?
Q: Why not a Transformer / simpler baseline?
Q: How do you know the gain is not just from additional parameters?
Q: How did you avoid data leakage?
Q: How robust is the result under distribution shift?
Q: What assumptions does the method make?
Q: What is the strongest evidence that your interpretation is correct?
```

## Research Story

最終的には個々の project だけでなく、自分の研究全体を一貫した story として説明できるようにする。

- What scientific problems have I been trying to solve?
- What modeling ideas recur across my work?
- What did I learn from failures?
- Why move toward foundation models / agents / AI for Science?
- What research questions do I want to pursue next?
