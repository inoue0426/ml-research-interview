## Technical Defense

- [ ] What alternatives did you try?
- [ ] What are the strongest baselines?

- [ ] What assumptions are you making?
- [ ] Where can leakage/confounding occur?

## Attack Questions
1. Why not use a Transformer / simpler baseline?
2. Is the gain just from more parameters or compute?
3. What result would falsify your explanation?
4. What is the largest limitation?
5. What would you do differently now?
