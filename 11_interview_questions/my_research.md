# Interview Questions — My Research

## Research Story
- [ ] What is the central theme of your research?
- [ ] Which project best represents your research style?
- [ ] How has your research evolved?
- [ ] What do you want to work on next?

## For Every Major Project
- [ ] What problem did you solve and why does it matter?
- [ ] What was your hypothesis?
- [ ] What did you personally contribute?
- [ ] Why this architecture / objective / data representation?
- [ ] What is the strongest baseline?
- [ ] What ablation is most convincing?
- [ ] What is the biggest limitation?
- [ ] What failed?
- [ ] What would you change today?
- [ ] What follow-up experiment would you run with 10× compute/data?

## Technical Attacks
- [ ] Why not a Transformer?
- [ ] Why not a simpler model?
- [ ] Is the gain just more parameters / compute / side information?
- [ ] How do you rule out leakage?
- [ ] How robust is the result under distribution shift?
- [ ] Which biological assumptions are unverified?

Practice each project at 30 sec / 2 min / 10 min depth.
